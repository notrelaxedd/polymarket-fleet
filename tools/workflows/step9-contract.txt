1. HOW TRADING WORKS (daily, market-on-close)

- One decision per assignment per trading session. A decision is due when the broker
  clock says the market is open today and now >= next_close - stock_decision_lead_min
  minutes (default 20, so 15:40 New York on a normal day; half days follow next_close)
  and assignment.last_decision_date != session_date.
- Signals use ONLY daily bars dated strictly before session_date (bars through the
  previous session). No intraday data. This makes the live decision identical to the
  backtest decision.
- Orders are whole shares, type market, time_in_force "cls" (market on close), so they
  fill at today's closing price, exactly what the backtest assumes. Alpaca refuses cls
  orders after 15:50 New York, and a cls order cannot be cancelled after that either.
- Target: weights w[symbol] in [0,1], sum <= 1, from the model. Equity E = cash +
  reserved + sum(qty * ref_price). target_qty = floor(w * E / ref_price). ref_price =
  the previous session's adjusted close (cents). Orders = target_qty - held qty: sells
  first in the batch, then buys. Rationale one line: "momentum rank 2/20, w 0.25, target 12 held 8".
- Mode of a stock assignment = the broker environment (alpaca_credentials environment:
  paper keys on paper-api, live keys on api). paper assignments trade Alpaca's paper
  account; live assignments the real account. There is no host-simulated stock fill.

4. MODELS, BACKTEST, SEARCH (A)

fleet/stocks/families.py: class StockModel with family, PARAM_KEYS, __init__(params),
weights(hist: dict[str, list[Bar]], day: str) -> dict[str, float], where hist holds only
bars dated < day (the backtester slices; the model never sees day itself).
Families and search space (search.py samples them with random.Random(seed)):
- momentum: lookback 21..252, skip 0..21, top_k 1..10, rebalance_days 1..21,
  trend_sma 0 or 50..250 (SPY close below its SMA -> all cash; needs SPY in the data).
- meanrev: lookback 2..10, hold_days 1..10, top_k 1..10, long_sma 0 or 100..250
  (only names above their SMA).
- trend: fast 5..50, slow 50..250 (fast < slow), max_names 1..20, equal weight among
  names with SMA(fast) > SMA(slow).
- buyhold: symbol "SPY" (the benchmark; also assignable).
Weights are long only, each <= 1, sum <= 1, deterministic for the same input.

backtest.py: run(model, bars, symbols, first_year, last_year, cost_bps, should_stop, on_unit)
daily loop over the trading days of the years (dates of SPY or the union): decide at
day d from bars < d, trade at d's close with cost_bps per side on the traded value,
whole-share-free (fractional) in the backtest, start equity 1.0. Returns metrics
(metrics.py): cagr, ann_vol, sharpe (rf 0, 252 days), max_drawdown (positive
fraction), turnover (annual), trades (count of position changes), exposure (mean
invested fraction), days, per_year [{year, return, sharpe, max_drawdown}], benchmark
{same keys for SPY buy and hold}, first_day, last_day. Units for emit(): one per
model-year, each well under 3 s.

Job kinds (fleet/worker/stock_jobs.py, registered in fleet/worker/jobs.py JOBS):
- stock_search params {n, seed, families (list, default all four), symbols, years
  [first,last], cost_bps, top_k (default 5)}: samples n candidates, backtests each on
  the search years, result {"create_stock_models": [{family, params, params_hash
  (fleet.models.base.params_hash), summary (two sentences, plain words), backtest_metrics}]}
  for the top_k by sharpe with trades >= 10. Checkpoint every candidate-year.
- stock_backtest params {model: {family, params}, symbols, years, cost_bps, model_id}:
  result {"model_id", "backtest_metrics"} (informational; never overwrites the
  search-era metrics of a stored model with years inside the validation era: host
  refuses such a job, and the worker refuses it too).
- stock_validate params {model_id, model: {family, params}, symbols, years
  (= stock_validation_years resolved by the host), cost_bps}: result {"model_id",
  "validation_metrics"}. Refused when the search years overlap the validation years.
The host resolves [first, null] to the last complete calendar year and copies symbols
(the stock_symbols setting at creation) and cost_bps into params. Bars come from the
worker's stock bar cache; the runner context gives its path as params["_context"]
["stock_bars_path"] (B adds it to the job context the same way games_path is added).

5. HOST: MODELS, GATES, ASSIGNMENTS, APPROVAL (B)

stock_models rows come from stock_search results (UNIQUE family+params_hash: an
existing row is kept, its id returned). lineage_id = own id. validation_metrics from
stock_validate. Status (host/stocks/eligibility.py recompute(conn, model_id), called
after every new result, thresholds change, paper mark, and at host start):
- candidate -> paper_ok when backtest sharpe >= min_sharpe, max_drawdown <= max_drawdown,
  trades >= min_trades, validation_metrics present with sharpe >= min_validation_sharpe.
- paper_ok -> live_eligible when its paper assignments have >= min_days marks, total
  paper return >= min_return and paper max drawdown <= max_drawdown (from stock_marks).
- demotion recomputes the same way; retired is set only by the owner and is final.
  A demotion out of live_eligible halts the model's live assignments (their open
  orders become cancel_requested).

Assignments (host/stocks/assignments.py):
  create_assignment(conn, model_id, mode, bankroll_cents, symbols, actor) -> dict
  halt_assignment(conn, assignment_id, reason, actor) -> dict   (open orders -> cancel_requested)
  resume_assignment(conn, assignment_id, actor) -> dict          (same gates as create)
  close_assignment(conn, assignment_id, actor) -> dict           (only with no positions and no open orders)
Create rules: model status paper_ok or live_eligible (live: live_eligible only);
mode must equal stock_broker_state.environment with keys_present and a broker check
within stock_broker_poll_s * 4 (else 400 naming why); live also needs
settings.live_enabled; symbols a non-empty subset of instruments with tradable true
and bars; at most stock_max_assignments active+halted; bankroll_cents <= the broker's
cash_cents minus the bankrolls of the other active assignments of that mode (one
Alpaca account is shared). cash_cents = bankroll_cents. It creates the stock_trade job
(kind stock_trade, role trade, params {"assignment_id"}, max_expiries NULL like trade
jobs) and stores job_id. Audit rows for every owner action.

Worker routes (host/api/stocks.py worker_router; lease token auth like /trade/state):
- GET /api/v1/stock_trade/state?job_id=... -> {"kill", "assignment" {id, mode, status,
  symbols, cash_cents, reserved_cents, last_decision_date}, "model" {id, family, params},
  "positions" {symbol: qty}, "open_orders" [...], "broker" {environment, market_open,
  session_date, next_close, checked_at}, "decision" {"due": bool, "session_date",
  "ref_prices_cents" {symbol: cents}, "bars_through" date}, "settings" {stock_trade_tick_s,
  stock_decision_lead_min}}. ref prices = the last stock_bars close dated before
  session_date; decision.due false when bars_through is not the previous session.
- POST /api/v1/stock_orders/request {"job_id", "assignment_id", "session_date",
  "orders": [{"client_request_id", "symbol", "side", "qty", "ref_price_cents", "rationale"}]}
  -> {"orders": [{"client_request_id", "order_id", "status", "reason"}]}. Idempotent per
  (assignment_id, client_request_id). Marks assignment.last_decision_date = session_date
  even when every order is rejected (one decision per session).
- POST /api/v1/stock_trade/release {"job_ids"}: cancels the approved (not yet submitted)
  orders of those jobs' assignments, bounded and idempotent.
Owner JSON routes (owner_router, owner auth and Origin check like owner_trading):
POST /api/stocks/assignments, /api/stocks/assignments/{id}/halt, /resume, /close,
POST /api/stocks/models/{id}/retire, POST /api/stocks/jobs (create stock_search,
stock_backtest, stock_validate with host-filled params), GET /api/stocks/summary.

Approval (host/stocks/approve.py approve(conn, assignment, order, now) -> (status, reason)),
under SELECT ... FOR UPDATE of the assignment, in this order, first failing code wins:
  kill (kill_switch on) | halted (assignment not active) | environment (mode !=
  broker environment or keys absent) | broker_stale (checked_at older than 4 *
  stock_broker_poll_s) | live_disabled | not_live_eligible (live) | market_closed
  (broker says not a trading session) | moc_cutoff (now later than next_close - 11
  minutes) | session (session_date != broker session_date) | symbol (not in the
  assignment's symbols or instrument not tradable) | short (sell qty > held qty minus
  qty already in open sells) | max_order (qty * ref_price > stock_max_order_cents) |
  max_position (held + open buys + qty, times ref_price, > stock_max_position_cents) |
  cash (buy: qty * ref_price * (1 + stock_price_band) > cash_cents) | daily_loss
  (buys only: today's loss from the previous mark, cash + reserved + positions at ref
  prices, beyond stock_max_daily_loss_cents[mode]).
Approved buy: reserved = ceil(qty * ref_price * (1 + band)) moves cash -> reserved on
the order and the assignment. Sells reserve nothing. Every status change writes a
stock_order_events row (actor "host", "worker:<id>", "exchange", "owner:<login>").

6. EXCHANGE (C)

alpaca_trading.py AlpacaTrading(creds, http=None): place(order_row) -> exchange_order_id
(POST /v2/orders {symbol, qty: str, side, type "market", time_in_force "cls",
client_order_id: order id}), cancel(exchange_order_id) -> bool (DELETE /v2/orders/{id};
422 "cannot be cancelled" -> False), order_by_client_id(client_id) (GET
/v2/orders:by_client_order_id?client_order_id=), orders(status, after) (GET /v2/orders),
positions() (GET /v2/positions), account() (GET /v2/account), clock() (GET /v2/clock).
All errors redacted; 401/403 -> auth error; 429 -> backoff; timeouts never resubmit blind.

Tasks in host/exchange/main.py (all no-ops without Alpaca keys):
- stock_broker every stock_broker_poll_s: account + clock -> stock_broker_state
  (environment from the credentials, cents from dollar strings rounded half up,
  session_date = the New York date of next_close when market_open, else of next_open;
  last_error on failure). Then reconciliation: per mode == environment, the sum of
  stock_positions over assignments vs Alpaca positions; differences and Alpaca open
  orders that are not ours go into warnings (live: also host/kill.auto_kill with
  reasons stock_position_mismatch / unknown_order; paper: warnings only).
- stock_executor every stock_orders_poll_s (and right after a decision): approved ->
  submitting -> place -> open with exchange_order_id; a timeout stays submitting and is
  reconciled by order_by_client_id (found -> open; not found after 60 s -> expired with
  release); cancel_requested -> cancel at Alpaca -> cancelled (release) or, when Alpaca
  says it cannot be cancelled, stays open with reason "cancel refused (after 15:50)".
  Poll open/partial orders: filled_qty and filled_avg_price changes -> fills booked by
  stock_fills.book_fill; canceled/expired/rejected at Alpaca -> cancelled/expired/
  rejected_by_exchange with the reservation released. exchange_fill_id =
  "<exchange_order_id>:<cumulative filled_qty>".
- stock_fills.book_fill(conn, order_id, qty, price, exchange_fill_id, ts) -> bool, one
  savepoint: buy: cost_cents = round half up(qty * price * 100); reserved of the order
  and the assignment drop by the proportional reservation, cash takes back the
  unused part; position qty += qty, cost_cents += cost. sell: cash += proceeds,
  realized += proceeds - average cost of the qty sold, position qty -= qty, cost
  reduced proportionally. Idempotent on exchange_fill_id. A sell larger than the
  position or a fill on a closed order -> no booking, return False, auto-kill
  late_fill for live, warning for paper.
- stock_marks once per session after next_close + 30 min (New York): for every
  active/halted assignment equity = cash + reserved + sum(qty * that session's close
  from stock_bars, or Alpaca's position current price when the bar is missing) ->
  stock_marks (idempotent), then eligibility.recompute for the models with paper marks
  (B's function).
CLI: stock-smoke --confirm "STOCK SMOKE YYYY-MM-DD" (one 1-share SPY cls buy on the
current environment, cancelled after 10 s, refused after 15:45 New York),
stock-cancel-all --direct (cancel every open order on the Alpaca account and close
our rows), both with the kill and live gates of docs/LIVE.md.

7. WORKER TRADE TICK (A)

fleet/worker/stock_trade.py StockTradeLoop: every stock_trade_tick_s per held
stock_trade job: GET state; nothing under kill, when the assignment is not active, or
when decision.due is false; else build the batch from the model's weights on the
cached bars (refresh the cache first; if the cache's last date is older than
decision.bars_through, fetch again and skip this tick if still older), POST it once
(client_request_id = sha256(job_id, session_date, symbol, side)[:24]), log the
answer. Release on role change via /api/v1/stock_trade/release. A stock_trade job
counts as one trade slot.
