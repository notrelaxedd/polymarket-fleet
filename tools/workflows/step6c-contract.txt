Shared contract for step 6 Part C (in-game), feed and model parts. Field names are fixed.

0. Corrections to docs/INGAME.md that this contract adopts (the docs builder writes them into INGAME.md):
- games has no `espn` column; the ESPN event id is games.raw->>'espn' (indexed by migration 0009).
- ESPN is polled every gamestate_poll_s (default 4 s, allowed 3..5) per live game, not every 10 s; the total ESPN request rate stays at or under gamestate_max_rps (default 1.0 per second) by stretching the per-game interval when several games are live (interval = max(gamestate_poll_s, n_live_games / gamestate_max_rps)).
- Yahoo is an opt-in cross-check: gamestate_sources defaults to ["espn"]; "yahoo" is used only when listed AND yahoo_pbp_url is set (after the owner's probe confirms the payload).
- pbp_rows.yardline_100 is the distance to the opponent's end zone for the team in possession (nflverse's own yardline_100 and ESPN's yardsToEndzone); the model derives the home-perspective field position from it and posteam_is_home.
- In-game orders are paper-only in this step (approval reason "ingame_paper_only" for live); a live in-game gate is a later owner decision.

1. Migration host/migrations/0009_ingame.sql is on disk (owned by the orchestrator; ask in your report for any change). It creates game_state, feed_lag, pbp_rows, the games espn index, assignments.ingame_model_id and trade_ingame, orders.ingame, bets.ingame and state_at_entry, model_scores.ingame_n_bets and ingame_pnl_cents, and the settings trade_ingame, ingame_tick_s, ingame_max_state_age_s, ingame_quiet_seconds, ingame_cutoff_seconds, ingame_dead_zone, ingame_min_edge, ingame_max_bet_cents, ingame_gtd_seconds, ingame_max_lag_s, gamestate_poll_s, gamestate_max_rps, gamestate_sources, espn_summary_url (template with {event_id}), yahoo_pbp_url, yahoo_poll_s.

2. Game state (one row per observation; the in-game model's input):
- game_state row: game_id, ts (host arrival time, UTC), source ("espn_summary" | "espn_scoreboard" | "yahoo"), event_ts (the play's wallclock when the source gives one), status ("pre" | "in" | "half" | "end_period" | "final"), period (1-4, 5+ overtime), clock_seconds (seconds left in the period), home_score, away_score, possession ("home" | "away" | null), down, distance, yardline_100 (possession-team perspective), home_timeouts, away_timeouts, play_id (the source's play id; deduplicated per source), play_text, raw (the parsed source fragment, truncated to 8 KB).
- The state dict used everywhere (parsers, model, trade payload) has exactly the keys: {"status", "period", "clock_seconds", "home_score", "away_score", "possession", "down", "distance", "yardline_100", "home_timeouts", "away_timeouts"}.
- host/exchange/gamestate.py public API:
  - parse_summary(payload) -> list[dict] (state dicts plus "play_id", "play_text", "event_ts"; the current situation as the last element; empty on anything unparseable, never raises)
  - parse_scoreboard_states(payload) -> dict[event_id, state dict] (fallback when the summary has no plays yet)
  - poll(conn, now, fetch=None, rng=None) -> dict summary {"polled": n, "rows": n, "backoff_until": iso|null, "errors": [...]}; fetch(url) -> (status, text) injectable for tests (default host.exchange.adapters.base.http_get); polls only games with an active or halted assignment whose kickoff has passed and that are not final; per-game cadence and the global rate as in section 0; on 429 or 403: jittered exponential backoff for that source (15 s doubling to 300 s), never a tight retry; failures are logged and leave the state stale.
  - latest_state(conn, game_id) -> dict | None: the newest row as {"state": state dict, "ts", "age_s", "source", "last_change": {"kind": "score"|"possession", "ts"} | None}.
  - lag_status(conn, source=None) -> {"suspended": bool, "median_lag_s": float | None, "n": int, "by_source": {source: {...}}}: over the last 20 feed_lag rows with a market_moved_at; suspended when the median lag (feed_seen_at - market_moved_at, positive = feed behind the market) exceeds settings.ingame_max_lag_s.
- host/exchange/feedlag.py: record events (a score change or a possession change seen first by a source) into feed_lag and later fill market_moved_at = the first price_snapshots.ts (for either of the game's confirmed markets) at or after (event_ts or feed_seen_at) - 120 s where the mid differs by more than 0.03 from the last mid before that window start; lag_s = feed_seen_at - market_moved_at in seconds.
- ExchangeLoop gains task "gamestate" (interval 1.0 s, its own cadence inside) after "snapshots" in ORDER.
- Exchange CLI: `probe-gamestate --event <espn id> [--yahoo]` prints the HTTP status and the first 64 KB of the payload plus what the parser extracted (never raises), and a dashboard button "Probe game state" next to "Probe markets" that renders the same through probe.html.

3. Play-by-play rows (training data for ingame_wp):
- pbp_rows row (and feed row): {"game_id", "play_id", "season", "home_win" (1, 0.5, 0), "score_diff" (home minus away before the play), "seconds_remaining" (game seconds left, 3600 at kickoff, 0 at the end of regulation; overtime plays use the overtime clock with half 3), "half" (1, 2, 3 = overtime), "down", "ydstogo", "yardline_100", "posteam_is_home", "home_timeouts", "away_timeouts", "pregame_p_home" (devigged closing moneyline of the game from games, null when missing), "vegas_wp" (nflverse vegas_home_wp)}.
- Host ingest host/pbp_rows.py streams nflverse play_by_play_{season}.csv.gz (gzip + csv, never the whole season in memory), keeps plays with a down or a kickoff/timeout state that has a clock, upserts idempotently; CLI `ingest-pbp-rows --season N` (new subcommand in a new module host/cli_ingame.py wired from host/cli.py's dispatcher with one line).
- Worker feed GET /api/v1/data/pbp?seasons=2012-2025 (new router module host/api/data_pbp.py mounted in host/api/app.py; worker auth; ETag = count + max season + a stamp; body is gzip-compressed JSON lines, one row per line, Content-Encoding not used: Content-Type application/x-ndjson+gzip so urllib does not decompress it).
- Worker cache: fleet/worker/pbp_cache.py (new): fetch with ETag into the data dir as pbp.jsonl.gz, iterate rows lazily.

4. Model family ingame_wp (fleet/models/ingame_wp.py, stdlib):
- class IngameWP with params {"l2" (search log-uniform [0.01, 10]), "time_scale" (search [0.5, 2.0]), "fp_scale" (search [0.5, 2.0])}; features(state, pregame_p) -> list[float] (constant 1.0; score_diff / sqrt(seconds_remaining/3600 + 0.01) * time_scale; logit(pregame_p) and logit(pregame_p) * seconds_remaining/3600; possession sign (home +1, away -1, none 0) times field position (100 - yardline_100)/100 * fp_scale; down and distance terms for the team in possession signed by possession; timeout difference; end-of-half indicators for the last 120 s of each half); fit(rows) with fleet.models.newton.fit_logistic (intercept unpenalised); predict(state, pregame_p) -> p_home clamped to [0.001, 0.999]; to_json/from_json round trip; summary() three sentences.
- pbp row to state: status "in", period = 1 + (3600 - seconds_remaining) // 900 for half 1 and 2 (5 for half 3), clock_seconds = seconds_remaining % 900 (or the overtime clock), home_score/away_score from score_diff (home_score = max(score_diff, 0), away_score = max(-score_diff, 0) suffices for the model which uses only the difference), possession from posteam_is_home.
- fleet/sim/ingame.py: run_ingame_search(rows_iter_factory, params_grid or n candidates + seed, train_seasons, validation_seasons, emit, should_stop, checkpoint) -> {"top": [...], "models": [{"family": "ingame_wp", "params", "artifact", "validation": {...}}]} where validation = {"n_plays", "log_loss", "vegas_log_loss", "beats_baseline": bool (log_loss <= vegas_log_loss), "by_period": {"1": {...}, ...}, "by_score_bucket": {"<=-9": ..., "-8..-1": ..., "0": ..., "1..8": ..., ">=9": ...}, "calibration": ten buckets}; fit only on train seasons (never the validation era); checkpoint per candidate.

5. Owner rules for this part: free data only (ESPN unofficial endpoints, nflverse); no broadcast capture; polite polling (the rate cap above); every game_state row and feed_lag row logged; stale state blocks trading (enforced later by the trading part).

==== Part 2: the trading side of in-game (step 6C main build). Field names are fixed. ====

6. Orchestrator decisions (apply them; they amend sections 0-5):
- ingame_wp: the L2 penalty is per 1000 plays (effective l2 * n_plays / 1000) so the search range matters; time_scale is an exponent: score_diff / (t + 0.01) ** (0.5 * time_scale). Update fleet/models/ingame_wp.py and its tests.
- lag_status suspends only with at least ingame_lag_min_events (new setting, default 5, add it to migration 0009) measured events; fewer events = not suspended, reported as "not enough data".
- A postponed or cancelled game (ESPN post, not completed) is final for the feed: polling and in-game trading stop.
- The in-game search returns "create_models" like other searches so the agent posts them: each entry {"family": "ingame_wp", "params", "artifact", "backtest_metrics": selection metrics with "era": "search", "validation_metrics": the validation dict with "era": "validation"}. Job kind model_search with params family "ingame_wp", train_seasons, validation_seasons, n, seed, top_k, train_fraction (host defaults [2012, 2021], [2022, null], 20, 1, 5, 0.3).
- Eligibility for ingame_wp lineages: paper_ok when validation_metrics.beats_baseline is true and n_plays >= 10000, otherwise candidate; never live_eligible in this step. In-game orders are paper-only: approval rejects a live in-game order with reason "ingame_paper_only".

7. Assignments: assignments.ingame_model_id (nullable, must be an ingame_wp model) and assignments.trade_ingame (default from settings.trade_ingame, false). POST /api/assignments and the New assignment form accept "ingame_model_id" and "trade_ingame"; PATCH-like owner route POST /api/assignments/{id}/ingame {"ingame_model_id", "trade_ingame"} toggles them on an existing assignment (audited).

8. Trade state payload (host/trading/state.py, per assignment): "ingame": {"enabled": bool (trade_ingame and an ingame model set), "model": {"id", "family", "params", "artifact"} | null, "game_state": latest_state(...) | null (state dict, ts, age_s, source, last_change), "pregame_p_home": devigged closing moneyline or the frozen closing mid of the home market, "lag": lag_status(conn) summary {"suspended", "median_lag_s", "n"}}; STATE_SETTINGS gain ingame_tick_s, ingame_max_state_age_s, ingame_quiet_seconds, ingame_cutoff_seconds, ingame_dead_zone, ingame_min_edge, ingame_max_bet_cents, ingame_gtd_seconds.

9. Worker in-game rules (new fleet/worker/trade_ingame.py, called from the trade tick every ingame_tick_s for assignments with ingame.enabled once the game is in progress; pregame rules stay as they are before kickoff):
- skip unless game_state status "in", age_s <= ingame_max_state_age_s, not within ingame_quiet_seconds of last_change, game seconds remaining > ingame_cutoff_seconds (regulation: (4 - period) * 900 + clock; overtime: clock);
- p_home = ingame_wp.predict(state, pregame_p_home); |p_side - mid| < ingame_dead_zone -> nothing;
- buys as pre-game with ingame_min_edge instead of min_edge and an extra cap ingame_max_bet_cents, only when lag.suspended is false; sells (step 6B rule) with ingame_min_edge, allowed even when suspended;
- requests carry "ingame": true and "gtd_seconds": ingame_gtd_seconds; client_request_id includes "ingame".

10. Host approval for in-game requests (new host/trading/ingame.py, dispatched from limits/sells when body.ingame is true): reasons in order after kill/lease/assignment/market: "ingame_disabled" (assignment not enabled), "ingame_paper_only" (live mode), "ingame_stale" (no state or older than ingame_max_state_age_s), "ingame_quiet" (within quiet seconds of the last score or possession change), "ingame_cutoff" (inside the cutoff), "ingame_lag_suspended" (buys only), "max_bet" also against ingame_max_bet_cents; then the usual stale book, liquidity, participation, price band, bankroll, daily loss (buys), exposure checks. The pre-game "kickoff" rejection does not apply to in-game requests. orders.ingame = true; the order's GTD is ingame_gtd_seconds.
- Executor: cancel_at_kickoff skips in-game orders; expire paper in-game orders after their GTD. Paper fills: kickoff_bound does not stop in-game orders (they fill on snapshots after kickoff while open).

11. Scoring and settlement: bets of in-game orders carry ingame true and state_at_entry (the game_state at approval: {period, clock_seconds, home_score, away_score, possession}); their clv is null (CLV excludes in-game rows); model_scores.ingame_n_bets and ingame_pnl_cents are maintained (n_bets and pnl keep counting everything as before, CLV only pre-game buys). Scores are kept for the in-game model's lineage too: an in-game order's bets row is attributed to the ingame_model_id (model_id/lineage_id of that model), so the ingame_wp lineage gets a paper record.
