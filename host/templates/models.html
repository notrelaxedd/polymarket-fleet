One row per lineage. Ranked on paper CLV (shrunk, ties by ROI) once a lineage has 5 paper games and 30 paper bets, then on snapshot replay CLV (shrunk, ties by ROI) once a lineage has 30 bets replayed on recorded prices, otherwise on the held-out validation era: shrunk ROI (ROI x bets / (bets + 100)), ties by the log-loss gain over the market. CLV is the closing price minus the price paid: above zero means the market moved the model's way after it bought. A lineage keeps its search-era numbers muted beside them; "beats market" is a sign-flip p below 0.05.

In-game models ingame_wp, judged on held-out plays against the vegas_wp baseline; paper only
